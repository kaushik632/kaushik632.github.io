Bamboo Nano Hat Liner

An integrated product & process design project that took a reusable cap liner from user research through CAD engineering, FMEA risk analysis, and financial modeling — validating a functional 3D-printed prototype at $7.50 per unit.

Executive Summary

Designed a three-layer reusable cap liner using bamboo fiber, SAP nano-absorbent core, and perforated TPU to solve sweat buildup, odor, and cap damage. Conducted user research across athletes, students, and everyday cap wearers, then validated the concept through a functional prototype, full FMEA analysis (12 failure modes, highest RPN 270), and NPV-driven financial modeling with a total investment of $15,200.

01 — The Problem

Caps Get Ruined From the Inside Out

Sweat and odor buildup from everyday use degrades caps from the inside. Washing damages the shape and fades the color. Bacteria growth causes irritation and persistent smell — making users hesitant to wear their best caps.

Existing solutions (disposable sweat liners, sprays, stick-on pads) are either single-use, ineffective, or change how the cap looks and feels. Users told us they wanted something that works without being noticeable.

02 — User Research

What Users Actually Care About

We interviewed three user segments — student cap users, athletes, and casual everyday wearers — to understand the real pain points and priorities that would drive adoption.

User research synthesis — three target segments mapped to five core feedback themes driving product requirements.
Hygiene & Freshness
Users strongly valued odor prevention. Several said odor made them hesitant to wear their best caps.
Comfort
Must be soft, breathable, and not bulky. Any added thickness that changes the fit is a dealbreaker.
Appearance
Users don't want the liner to change how the cap looks. Invisible integration is critical.
Ease of Use
Insertion and removal must be quick. Washing should be simple — no special care instructions.

The core insight: users want a product that solves the problem without them having to think about it. Convenience and invisibility matter more than raw performance specs.

03 — Three-Layer Design

Engineered From the Inside Out

Each layer of the liner serves a distinct function. The layered architecture balances sweat absorption, odor control, breathability, and structural support in a single removable insert.

Layered material architecture — each layer serves a distinct functional role in the sweat management system.
Layer 01
Bamboo / Cotton Blend
Soft, skin-friendly material that absorbs sweat while preventing fabric abrasion against the cap interior.
Layer 02
SAP Absorbent Sheet
Locks in moisture and prevents sweat from bleeding through the hat band. Super-absorbent polymer core.
Layer 03
Perforated TPU
Provides airflow and slight rigidity to maintain the cap's crown shape. Perforations optimize ventilation.
04 — How It Works

Simple. Reusable. Invisible.

Functional prototype — 3D-printed dome liner inserted inside a standard cap, with nano fabric close-up showing perforation pattern.
  1. Liner is inserted inside the inner crown of the cap
  2. Bamboo framework maintains the dome structure
  3. Nano fabric absorbs sweat and suppresses odor during wear
  4. After use, liner is removed, washed, and reused
  5. Hat stays clean, dry, and structurally intact
